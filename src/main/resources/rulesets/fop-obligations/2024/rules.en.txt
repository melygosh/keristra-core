Hit Policy: FIRST
Determine the monthly payments of a FOP who pays the single tax in groups 1–3 in 2024.
Single tax (apply the maximum rates): group 1 — 10% of the subsistence minimum for able-bodied persons set on 1 January of the year (UAH 3,028 in 2024); group 2 — 20% of the minimum wage set on 1 January of the year (UAH 7,100 in 2024); group 3 — 5% of the monthly income, or 3% of the monthly income for a VAT payer. For reference: from 1 April 2024 the minimum wage is UAH 8,000.
Single-tax payers do not pay the military levy in 2024.
For groups 1 and 2 the payments do not depend on the income. VAT registration affects only the group 3 single-tax rate.
Amounts are rounded to kopecks (2 decimal places).
The result is stored in the variable singleTax (UAH per month). If no group is selected, the result stays empty.
