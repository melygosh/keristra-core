Hit Policy: FIRST
Determine the monthly payments of a FOP who pays the single tax in groups 1–3 in 2025.
Single tax (apply the maximum rates): group 1 — 10% of the subsistence minimum for able-bodied persons set on 1 January of the year (UAH 3,028 in 2025); group 2 — 20% of the minimum wage set on 1 January of the year (UAH 8,000 in 2025); group 3 — 5% of the monthly income, or 3% of the monthly income for a VAT payer.
Military levy: for groups 1 and 2, 10% of the minimum wage set on 1 January of the year; for group 3, 1% of the monthly income regardless of VAT registration.
For groups 1 and 2 the payments do not depend on the income. VAT registration affects only the group 3 single-tax rate.
Amounts are rounded to kopecks (2 decimal places).
The result is stored in the variables singleTax and militaryLevy (UAH per month). If no group is selected, the result stays empty.
